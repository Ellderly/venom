Warfront sounds are built from these recordings (edited, layered, filtered, compressed):

- Sunset Sound Effects Library (USC Cinema / Sunset Editorial), CC0 1.0, via the Internet Archive:
  real explosions, atomic bomb recordings, incoming and overhead artillery shells,
  PT boat rapid-fire guns and a .50 cal machine gun (the Shilka anti-aircraft gun); V-2 and small rocket
  launches (S-400, Strela, Grad).
  https://archive.org/details/SSE_Library_EXPLOSIONS  https://archive.org/details/SSE_Library_GUNS
  https://archive.org/details/SSE_Library_AIRCRAFT
- BigSoundBank.com, CC0 1.0, recordings by Joseph SARDIN: explosion (#1807, #1808), thunder (#2718),
  excavator (#2148), fall of stone (#1022), firecracker (#1137), DJI quadcopter drone (#3551, base of the FPV
  drone motor sound); cab sounds (0.30): car door opening (#1524), car door slam (#0655), metal box lid as a hatch
  (#0053), pneumatic brake release (#1490), truck reverse beep (#1298). https://bigsoundbank.com
- NASA audio (public domain; NASA does not endorse this mod): SLS test fire, Discovery STS-131 launch,
  Atlas V launch. https://www.nasa.gov/historical-sounds/
- Geran-2 engine: a real Shahed-136 recording over a city, zvukogram.com ("Звук Шахеда-136 в небе над городом").
- FPV drone (close whine, distant buzz, prop wash) and Lancet loitering munition (motor, distant buzz, dive whistle,
  impact, catapult whoosh): recordings from zvukogram.com, "Звуки дрона" ("Резкий жужжащий пролёт скоростного дрона",
  "Скоростной квадрокоптер проносится через лес", "Проп-вош на полной мощности", "Заход кружащего ударника на цель",
  "Далёкое жужжание в небе на патруле", "Свистящий разгон гонки в лесу").
- Synthesized: electronic beeps, MANPADS seeker tones, rangefinder clicks and radio report, turbine whine,
  sub-bass of explosions and gunshots, FPV motor whine layer, FPV arming beeps and goggles beep, air raid siren wail,
  surveillance radar antenna drive.
